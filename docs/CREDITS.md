## Drawn for this app

All other files in `app/img/signs/`, plus everything in `app/img/signals/` and `app/img/diagrams/`, are generated by `tools/build-images.js` using the shapes, colors, and wording shown in the California Driver's Handbook sign charts (Section 7). Pictograms are simplified drawings.

## Logo

`app/img/logo.svg` and `logo.ico`: original logo made for this app (not a DMV or State of California mark).

## Handbook PDF

`app/handbook/california-driver-handbook.pdf`: California Driver’s Handbook, California Department of Motor Vehicles, licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Edition DL 600 R6/2025, downloaded 2026-11-15 from https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/file/california-driver-handbook-pdf/ . Non-commercial use only.

`california-driver-handbook-highlighted.pdf` and the page images in `app/handbook/pages/`: made from the same handbook. The highlighted PDF has yellow highlight annotations (and a note on page 1) added by `tools/highlight-handbook.py`; the page images are rendered by `tools/build-handbook-pages.py`, and the in-app highlights are drawn on top of them. None of the highlights or notes are part of the DMV publication.
